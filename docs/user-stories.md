# User stories

## Officer
1. As an officer, I see the files assigned to me, so I know my workload.
2. As an officer, I see files due today at the top of my home screen.
3. As an officer, I mark a stage complete and the file moves to the next stage with the right due date.
4. As an officer, I schedule an EVC visit and get reminders as the date approaches.
5. As an officer, I record an undertaking with a due date so it is tracked automatically.
6. As an officer, I view an uploaded proof and accept or reject it with remarks.

## Supervisor
7. As a supervisor, I see overdue and escalated items from my team in one list.
8. As a supervisor, I open any team file and see its full stage history.

## Head office
9. As head office, I see pending files by region and stage, and where files get stuck.
10. As head office, I edit alert timings and recipients without a code change.
11. As head office, I run the daily check on demand and see the alerts it produces.
12. As head office, I export a compliance report with the full history of each item.

## Institution
13. As an institution user, I submit an application and see its current stage.
14. As an institution user, I see my promises to submit, upload proof online, and see documents about to expire.
15. As an institution user, I upload a renewed document before the old one expires.
